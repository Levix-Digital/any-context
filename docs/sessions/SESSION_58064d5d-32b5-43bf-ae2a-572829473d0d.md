### 2. Milestone 3 (Release `v0.30.7`) — Universal `StructuredDataChunker`
- **Inviolable Global Product Guideline (Calgary, Alberta, Canada)**:
  - AnyContext is a global product conceived in Calgary, Alberta, Canada. It has no, and will have no, regionalized rules, models, calculations, legislation or tax formats of any country.
  - Structured parsing works exclusively on the universal open standards W3C XML, ECMA-404 JSON and YAML 1.2.
- **Universal XML (`xml.rs`)**:
  - Event-based reading with `quick-xml = "0.37"`.
  - Builds complete syntactic spans of XML nodes, guaranteeing tags are closed.
  - Extracts hierarchical breadcrumbs: `// Context: <file>.xml > root > parent > item`.
  - Groups repeated sibling nodes (e.g. `<item>`, `<dependency>`, `<record>`) up to `max_chunk_chars`.
  - Resilient fallback through `splitter::split_oversized_code` against huge nodes or malformed XML.
- **Universal JSON & JSONL (`json.rs`)**:
  - Dual support for JSON Lines (`.jsonl`, `.ndjson`) with line windowing, and standard `.json`.
  - Hierarchical decomposition of arrays and objects with indentation and key grouping (`// Context: <file>.json > root > {key1, key2}`).
  - Resilient fallback through `split_oversized_code`.
- **Universal YAML (`yaml.rs`)**:
  - Multi-document detection and splitting (`---`).
  - Smart extraction of resource labels (e.g. `Deployment: api`, `Service: web`).
  - Slicing of mappings and sequences through `serde_yaml = "0.9"`.
- **Orchestrator & Router (`mod.rs`, `router.rs`, `router.py`)**:
  - Registered in `IngestionRouter` for `.xml`, `.json`, `.jsonl`, `.ndjson`, `.yaml`, `.yml`.
- **LanceDB Taxonomy (`indexer.py`)**:
  - Automatic assignment of `XML Structured Document`, `JSON Structured Data` and `YAML Configuration`.
