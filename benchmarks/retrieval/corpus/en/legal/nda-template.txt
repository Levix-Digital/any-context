Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement — Template Summary
Fictitious document of Kestrel Analytics.

Each party keeps the other party's confidential information secret for 3 years after the last disclosure. Trade secrets stay protected for as long as they remain trade secrets.

Confidential information may be shared only with employees and contractors who need it and who are bound by similar obligations.

The agreement is governed by the laws of the state named on the signature page. Either party may end it with 30 days written notice.
