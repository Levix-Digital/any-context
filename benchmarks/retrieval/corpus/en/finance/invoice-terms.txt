Invoice and Payment Terms
Fictitious document of Kestrel Analytics.

Invoices are payable within 30 days of the invoice date (net 30). Late payments accrue a fee of 1.5% per month on the open amount.

Accounts more than 60 days overdue are suspended after a written notice. Disputes must be raised within 15 days of the invoice date.

We accept bank transfer and corporate cards. Card payments over USD 10,000 need a prior agreement.
