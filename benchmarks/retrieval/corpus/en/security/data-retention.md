# Data Retention Policy

> Fictitious document of Kestrel Analytics.

- Application logs are kept for 90 days, then deleted.
- Audit logs are kept for 7 years.
- Customer data is deleted 30 days after the end of the contract, unless the customer asks for an export first.
- Database backups are kept for 21 days.

Deletion requests from a customer are completed within 10 business days and confirmed in writing.
