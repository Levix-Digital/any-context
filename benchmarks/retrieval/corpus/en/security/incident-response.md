# Security Incident Response

> Fictitious document of Kestrel Analytics.

## Severity levels

- SEV1: customer data exposed or the platform is down. The on-call engineer is paged and must acknowledge within 5 minutes.
- SEV2: a single feature is down or degraded. Acknowledge within 30 minutes.
- SEV3: no customer impact. Handle in business hours.

## Communication

For a SEV1, the status page is updated within 15 minutes and then every 30 minutes until resolution.

## Postmortem

Every SEV1 and SEV2 gets a written, blameless postmortem within 5 business days.
