# Support Escalation Matrix

> Fictitious document of Kestrel Analytics.

| Level | Who | First response |
|---|---|---|
| L1 | support desk | within 4 business hours |
| L2 | product specialists | next business day |
| L3 | engineering | agreed case by case |

A ticket moves from L1 to L2 when the support desk cannot reproduce it or when it needs a configuration change. Enterprise customers can escalate directly to L2 by phone.
