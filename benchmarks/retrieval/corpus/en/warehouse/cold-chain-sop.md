# Cold Chain Standard Operating Procedure

> Fictitious document of Kestrel Analytics' logistics partners program.

## Temperature range

Refrigerated goods must stay between 2 °C and 8 °C at all times. The temperature is logged automatically every 10 minutes.

## Excursions

A temperature excursion lasting more than 30 minutes puts the whole pallet in quarantine until quality assurance releases it. Shorter excursions are recorded and reviewed weekly.

## Loading

Refrigerated trucks must be pre-cooled for at least 45 minutes before loading. Doors stay open for no more than 10 minutes per stop.
