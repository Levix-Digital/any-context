# New Employee Onboarding Checklist

> Fictitious document of Kestrel Analytics.

1. Day 1: laptop, badge and accounts are ready; the manager introduces the team.
2. Within 7 days: complete the security awareness training and sign the acceptable use policy.
3. Within 30 days: meet with the onboarding buddy every week and agree on 90-day goals with the manager.
4. Day 90: probation review.

New employees do not get production access before the security training is completed.
