# Business Travel Policy

> Fictitious document of Kestrel Analytics.

## Flights

Economy class is required for flights shorter than 6 hours. Business class is allowed for flights of 6 hours or more, and for any overnight flight.

## Hotels

The hotel cap is USD 220 per night, or USD 300 in the cities on the high-cost list.

## Booking

All travel is booked through the travel desk at least 14 days in advance. Late bookings need the approval of a vice president.
