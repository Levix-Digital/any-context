# Pricing Plans

> Fictitious document of Kestrel Analytics. Prices are illustrative.

| Plan | Users | Data retention | Single sign-on |
|---|---|---|---|
| Starter | up to 5 | 30 days | no |
| Team | up to 50 | 1 year | no |
| Enterprise | unlimited | custom | yes (SAML and OIDC) |

Annual billing gives two months free. Downgrading keeps the data but hides dashboards over the plan limit.
